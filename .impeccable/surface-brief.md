# VoicePrompt desktop

Mode: Operate. Windows desktop, web-rendered through Tauri.

Users dictate into other applications and briefly manage their tasks, timers,
alarms and programming contests. Success means safe complete dictation and
actionable reminders without losing their place in the destination application.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A compact voice utility with one clear working canvas and a contextual
island; all seven existing tools remain visible in familiar sidebar navigation.

OWN-WORLD: Forest-charcoal surfaces, mint actions, restrained amber reminders,
Segoe UI typography, fine borders, crisp controls and consistent small radii.
The user approved layout A and the three-tab island concept on 26 October 2026.

STORY: Prepare, hold Right Ctrl, speak, release, and continue working. The island
also keeps a running focus countdown and surfaces alarms and contest reminders.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 196px labeled rail; Dictation title and local-engine status;
recording controls above the latest transcript. The 112x32 island expands on
click into Focus, Alarms and Notifications. Alerts stay quiet during dictation.

FORM: User-pinned approved concept A, superseding exploratory seed 6647653b.

waveforms are semantic code, not raster assets. Sample content is preview-only.
